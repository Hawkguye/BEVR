# 2-minute demo script

1. **Desktop or Quest, start in H³.** “This is hyperbolic 3-space. Five cubes meet at every edge — you can count them if you look along a glowing beam.”
2. **Walk forward (WASD / left stick).** “The lattice fans out exponentially. There is always more space than you expect.”
3. **Hit `2` / Euclidean.** “Same aesthetic, ordinary cubic grid. This is the control: parallels stay parallel.”
4. **Hit `P` for Parallel Walkers.** Watch the HUD distance. Stay in E³: the number is constant. Switch to `1`: it grows. Switch to `3`: it shrinks and the orbs meet.
5. **Hit `3` / spherical.** “Eight cubic cells — the tesseract. Walk straight. After a lap of 2πK metres you are home.”
6. **Triangle tool (`T`).** Place three points. Read the angle sum: 180° / less / more. In H³ point at the deficit: that *is* the area.
7. **K slider.** Drop K toward 2 m. “Smaller curvature radius, stronger curve — the same walk now wraps harder.”
8. **Snap turn + vignette.** Comfort is on; no artificial camera motion besides the 30° snap.

Close: “You did not look at a picture of non-Euclidean geometry. You walked it.”
